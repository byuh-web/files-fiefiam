This photo tour will show you places that most students in Brigham Young University of Hawaii usually go to. There are many places you can visit in BYUH, like Cannon Activities Center(CAC) it's used for big gathering for Devotional, games, activities etc... Each place has something unique to offer, where PCC shares about the cultures of the Pacific Island, and Banyan a dinning hall where you can sit and eat with your family and friends. the Temple is a peaceful and sacred place where you can go to for comfort or The library if you want some peace and quiet.
