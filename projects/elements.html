What's fun about Volleyball

Volleyball is a high energy team sport played with a ball and a net. The game needs 6 players, coach, and a referee to count everytime a team scores. The purpose of this game is Teamwork trusting each other to carry the team.

Playing Volleyball can be indoor or outdoor, as long as there is a net and a ball to play. It can be a Good Activity for getting to know people.

It requires immense focus and quick reflexes to keep the ball from hitting the floor. Every match starts with a powerful serve. Anyone can play volleyball but what's fun about it? It's that anyone & everyone can play.

There are 4 position in volleyball. There is a setter, middle, outsider, and a libero. All 4 are important when playing especially the libero running and passing the ball. Another word for it is a defensive player. They wear a different colord jersey to stand out from their teammates on the court.

The middle and ourside players are to score a point by spiking or tipping the ball to the other side of the net. They also block especially the three players close to the net.

Blocking is the first line of defense. Players must jump with their arms extended to stop the ball at the net.

"It takes a lot of hard work and dedication just like any pro sport. Especially for beach volleyball you don't have to be tall or as fast as other sports. You just have to have the skill." - Misty May Treanor
