Rainbow

Rainbows form when sunlight enters a water droplet, bends (refracts), reflects off the inside back of the droplet, and bends again as it exits. Because white sunlight is a mixture of all visible colors, the water droplet splits the light into separate colors, a process called dispersion

Why people like rainbow?

People love rainbows because they symbolize hope, renewal, and joy. Appearing directly after a storm, they represent the transition from hardship to brighter days.
